Last-minute buyer

Iván Solís

Buys with his friends, on the bus, the night before — not weeks ahead.

23 · Monterrey
Android, metered data
2–3 seats together
Debit or OXXO

First job, first independent nights out. The decision to go does not start on the ticketing site — it starts in a WhatsApp group. Someone pastes a link; Iván is the one holding a debit card or standing near an OXXO. His entire purchase window is about nine minutes, often with one signal bar. He trusts a screenshot he can send to the chat more than a confirmation email he will not open until later.

“We’re going, right? Just let me check there are still seats together — and that it doesn’t get ridiculous with the service fee.”

Jobs to be done

  • Get his whole group through the door in contiguous seats
  • Know the all-in price before committing to a seat map
  • Finish checkout under social pressure without looking lost
  • Show proof the ticket is real (live QR, not a forwarded JPEG)

What “done” looks like

Friends reply with fire emojis to his QR screenshot. Nobody argues about who sits where. At the gate the scanner beeps once. He never has to explain a failed OXXO payment in the chat.

Friction that ends the session

  • A label that says “Total” on basePrice only — fee appears one screen later
  • Seat map that fights one-thumb use on a mid-range phone
  • Hold timer running while the group is still voting
  • OXXO/SPEI paid, status unclear → “did it go through?”

Key moment

Friday 7:40 pm. Bus. One bar. Event is tomorrow. Chat is already asking “did you buy?” If the map stalls or the price jumps, he closes the tab and types “couldn’t get them.”

Verified product evidence (not an interview finding)

Mexico pricing policy in code says the advertised price must be the charged price, and POST /pricing/calculate-cart is already public. That helper runs on cart, checkout, and order pages — not on the event page, where the UI still labels a sum of basePrice as “Total.”

pricing.ts · EventPurchaseClient.tsx:658 · Opportunity O3 · Hypothesis H2

Must build for him

  • All-in price (fee + VAT) from first screen
  • Honest hold: server expiry, renew, release notice
  • OXXO/SPEI status in plain language
  • Live QR inside the order, not a static image

Success metrics

  • Mobile cart → pay conversion
  • % holds expired without purchase
  • Support: “I paid and got nothing”
  • Instrument event → cart → pay (needed to falsify H2)

Subject in context

Iván Solís

Friday 7:40 pm · city bus · one signal bar · show is tomorrow · buying 2–3 tickets for a WhatsApp group that is deciding in real time.

Hypothesis under test
H2 — He abandons because the fee is a surprise, not because the amount is high.

Sees

Environment

One signal bar. A seat map that paints in late. A number labeled “Total” that is still only the seat base — service fee and VAT have not entered the frame yet.

WhatsApp banners slide over the purchase UI. The bus jolts; his thumb is the only pointer he has. Attention is fragmented before price ever becomes the issue.

Hears

Social pressure

“Did you buy yet?” The group is voting without him: together or not at all. Someone pastes a reseller link. Someone else says another app looked cheaper.

He is not shopping alone. He is the designated buyer — card holder or the one near an OXXO — executing a consensus he does not fully control.

Thinks & feels

Inner state · mood ▼▼

“Are they playing me?” Fear of missing the night and fear of overpaying arrive together. He doubts a digital ticket will “count” at the door until he can screenshot something that looks official.

Urgency is social and technical at once: if the hold dies while the chat argues, the contiguous seats vanish and he fails the group.

Says & does

Observable behavior

Screenshots the screen and drops it in the chat — that image is his social proof. Compares the total to resale. Asks “yes or no?” before paying. If OXXO: finds a store, saves the reference, then refreshes status obsessively.

One hard failure is enough. He abandons and messages the group: “couldn’t get them.”

Pains

  • “Total” that was not all-in (fee + VAT later)
  • Hold clock vs. an undecided group chat
  • Seat map unusable on one thumb + weak data
  • Post-OXXO / SPEI ambiguity: “did it clear?”
  • Feeling cheated even when the fee is fair

Gains

  • Walk in with friends, seats together, no drama
  • No price bait-and-switch feeling
  • A QR that looks official in the chat
  • One-attempt checkout under pressure
  • Gate scan that just works

Product implications

  • O3 Show real total on event + map, not only in cart
  • Hold UX: visible renew + expiry warning
  • OXXO/SPEI copy in human language
  • Live QR on the order (not a forwardable image)
  • Instrument mobile funnel event → cart → pay to falsify H2
