2 · Jobs to be done

Lock contiguous seats for his group fast, with the full price known before seat selection, and a digital ticket he can trust at the gate.

  • Group Freeze contiguous seats before the chat changes its mind or the hold expires.
  • Price See all-in cost (base + fees + VAT) before entering the seat map.
  • Pay Clear, real-time confirmation for debit, OXXO, or SPEI.
  • Access Live QR inside the order that works at the door — not a forwarded screenshot.
Hypothesis H2 — focus for eng / product

He abandons because the fee is a surprise, not because the amount is high. Showing all-in price early should cut cart drop-off.

Evidence: EventPurchaseClient.tsx:658 labels basePrice as “Total” · calculate-cart already public in pricing.ts · Opportunity O3
KPI · Cart
Mobile cart → pay conversion on weak networks
KPI · Holds

3 · Critical frictions

FrictionRoot cause & impact
Late fee reveal Event page shows “Total” on basePrice only. Fee + VAT appear in cart. Reads as bait-and-switch → instant abandon. EventPurchaseClient.tsx:658 · pricing.ts
Heavy seat map Map struggles on one-thumb + weak data. Contiguous seats are hard to lock before the group moves on. web/components/seatmap · ? H2
Rigid hold Timer keeps running while he confirms in WhatsApp. Seats drop; he fails the group. HoldCountdown (honest clock exists; UX still feels punitive)
OXXO / SPEI fog Payment sent, status unclear. Anxiety + support load (“did it clear?”). DeferredPaymentPanel · reconcile-spei

4 · Product requirements

  1. 1
    All-in pricing from first screen
    Render base + fees + VAT on event and map — same number cart will charge. Kill the fake “Total”.
  2. 2
    Hold that survives group chat
    Visible renew + warning under 60s. Server expiry stays source of truth.
  3. 3
    Plain-language OXXO / SPEI status
    States like “Waiting for payment” / “Payment verified” — not bank jargon.
  4. 4
    Live QR on the order
    QR regenerates inside the order; static captures must not pass at the gate.

Last-minute buyer

Iván Solís

Fast ticket buy under weak mobile data and a group deciding in WhatsApp. Focus minute: fee surprise on the way to cart (journey phase 3).

Context
Friday 7:40 pm · bus · 1 signal bar · event tomorrow · 2–3 tickets

Thinks & feels

01
  • Anxious the seats will vanish if the map is slow.
  • Angry when fees appear only at the end — feels cheated even if the fee is fair.
  • Expects checkout to feel as fast as sending a WhatsApp message.
  • Afraid of paying via OXXO/SPEI and the system not registering the ticket in time.

Sees

02
  • Friends organizing last-minute plans in group chats.
  • Ticketing pages with seat maps that load poorly on phone data.
  • Event ads whose price jumps hard at checkout.
  • A “Total” label that is not actually the amount he will pay.

Hears

03
  • “You get the tickets — we’ll transfer you later.”
  • “That site always dies when it’s on sale.”
  • “Together or don’t bother.”
  • Someone pasting a reseller link as a “cheaper” option.

Says & does

04
  • Buys on mobile while commuting or multitasking.
  • Screenshots the screen and asks the chat “yes or no?” before paying.
  • Compares the new total to resale or another app.
  • Abandons immediately if the price jumps — then texts “couldn’t get them.”

Pains

  1. Losing contiguous seats to a hold that expires while the group debates.
  2. Surprise fees at the end that blow a tight monthly budget.
  3. Weak signal that blocks map use — or showing the QR at the gate.

Gains

  1. Final price visible from the first tap — no bait-and-switch feel.
  2. Immediate, unambiguous payment confirmation (debit / OXXO / SPEI).
  3. Ticket available when signal is bad: live QR on the order, usable at the door.
