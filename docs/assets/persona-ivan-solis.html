Last-minute buyer

Iván Solís

Fee surprise on the way to cart · journey phase 3 · WhatsApp group deciding in real time

Context
Fri 7:40 pm · bus · 1 bar · event tomorrow · 2–3 tickets

Thinks & feels

01
  • Seats will vanish if the map is slow.
  • Fees at the end feel like a scam — even when fair.
  • Checkout should feel as fast as WhatsApp.
  • Afraid OXXO/SPEI won’t register in time.

Sees

02
  • Last-minute plans forming in group chat.
  • Seat maps that crawl on phone data.
  • Event prices that jump at checkout.
  • A “Total” that isn’t what he’ll pay.

Hears

03
  • “You buy — we’ll transfer later.”
  • “That site dies on sale day.”
  • “Together or don’t bother.”
  • A reseller link pasted as “cheaper.”

Says & does

04
  • Buys on mobile while commuting.
  • Screenshots → “yes or no?” in chat.
  • Compares new total to resale.
  • Price jumps → abandons → “couldn’t get them.”

Pains

  1. Hold expires while the group debates → loses contiguous seats.
  2. Surprise fees blow a tight monthly budget.
  3. Weak signal blocks the map — or the QR at the gate.

Gains

  1. Final price from the first tap — no bait-and-switch.
  2. Unambiguous payment confirmation (debit / OXXO / SPEI).
  3. Live QR on the order, usable when signal is bad.
