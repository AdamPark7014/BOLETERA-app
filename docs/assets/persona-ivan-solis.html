User Persona

Iván Solís

He decides with his friends, on the bus, the night before.

“We’re going, right? Just let me check there are still seats together — and that it doesn’t get crazy with the service fee.”

What he wants

  • To walk in with his friends, seated together
  • The final price before he picks a seat
  • Proof that the ticket is real

What burns him

  • The service fee showing up only at the end
  • A seat map he can’t work with one thumb
  • The hold timer running while the group decides
  • Paying at OXXO with no idea if it went through
