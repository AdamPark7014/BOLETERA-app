User Persona

Iván Solís

The last-minute buyer

“We’re going, right? Just let me check there are still seats together — and that it doesn’t get ridiculous with the service fee.”

Iván buys for his friends, not for himself. He decides the night before, usually on the bus with one bar of signal. He is always the one holding the card or standing near an OXXO — and he trusts a screenshot to the group chat more than a confirmation email.

What he needs

  • Seats together for the whole group
  • The real price before he picks a seat
  • Checkout as fast as sending a message

What breaks it

  • Service fee that appears only at checkout
  • Seat map that doesn’t work with one thumb
  • Timer running out while the group still decides

Key moment

Friday, 7:40 pm. One bar of signal on the bus. The show is tomorrow. The group is asking if he bought yet. If the price jumps or the map stalls, he closes the tab and says he couldn’t get the tickets.

Iván Solís

The minute he discovers the price isn’t what he thought — on mobile, under pressure, with his group waiting.

Friday 7:40 pm · on the bus · one bar of signal · event tomorrow

Thinks & feels

  • “Are they trying to rip me off?”
  • Scared of missing out and of overpaying at the same time
  • Not sure a digital ticket will count at the door
  • Panicked the seats will disappear while the chat argues

Sees

  • One bar of signal and a map loading slowly
  • A price labeled “Total” that isn’t the final amount
  • WhatsApp messages popping over the checkout screen
  • Ads that looked cheaper than what he’s about to pay

Hears

  • “Did you buy yet?”
  • “They have to be together or don’t bother”
  • “You get them — we’ll pay you back”
  • Someone sharing a reseller link as a cheaper option

Says & does

  • Screenshots the screen and sends it to the group
  • Asks “yes or no?” before paying
  • Compares the total to other sites
  • Quits immediately if the price jumps — then texts “couldn’t get them”

Pains

  • Losing seats together because the timer ran out
  • Hidden fees that blow his monthly budget
  • Bad signal blocking the map or the QR at the gate

Gains

  • Seeing the real price from the first tap
  • Clear confirmation that payment went through
  • Walking in with friends, no drama at the door
